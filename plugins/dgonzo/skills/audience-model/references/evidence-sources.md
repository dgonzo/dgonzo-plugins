# Evidence sources for the Forensic pass

Behaviour is evidence. A stated preference is a claim to check. Use the row for the reader's type.

| Reader | Behaviour to look for | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| Exec | Which past asks got a fast yes | Email and Slack threads with David |
| Exec | Which updates got no reply | Email and Slack threads with David |
| Exec | What they asked first in the last review | Meeting notes or recordings |
| Exec | What they last funded or cut | Budget decisions and planning docs |
| Customer | What they last bought or renewed | CRM record or contract history |
| Customer | What they raised in support tickets | Support queue |
| Customer | Which features they use | Product analytics |
| Customer | Which tool they adopted last, and why | Sales call notes |
| Candidate | What they built and shipped | CV and public professional work |
| Candidate | What they asked about in interviews | Interview notes |
| Candidate | Why they left past roles | Interview notes |
| Team | What they pushed back on last | Retro notes, PR reviews and threads |
| Team | Which announcements they acted on | Follow-up tickets and replies |
| Team | Which channel they read | Reply rates by channel |

## Status tags

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Observed | Seen in a record above |
| Stated | The reader said it, with no behaviour to confirm it |
| Assumed | No source, so it stays a gap |

A stated claim that contradicts observed behaviour is dropped from the model. Record the contradiction in the Evidence table.
